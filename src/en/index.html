Frequently asked

Things you'd ask anyway.

Five answers we usually give in the first call.

How much does a website from Karriaro cost? +

Four tiers, segmented by industry: Essential €1,290 (hairdresser, café, florist, gym), Professional €1,990 (roofer, logistics, physiotherapy, hotel), Premium €2,990 (dentist, doctor, real estate, lawyer), Premium+ €3,990 (lawyer with GDPR compliance — pilot). Maintenance optional from €99/month (Care) or €199/month (Care+). Source code belongs to you — no vendor lock-in.

How long does it take to build my website? +

You receive a draft within 24 hours. The finished website goes live in 7–14 days, depending on package. You only pay when you're satisfied.

Do I get industry-specific tools on my website? +

Yes. From the Professional tier we include one industry tool light (e.g. booking widget or price calculator). The Premium tier includes the full set: BAFA grant calculator for roofing, mortgage calculator with amortisation plan for real estate, AR try-on for hair salons, AI symptom checker for medical practices, AI sommelier for restaurants, capacity pulse for logistics — depending on industry.

Why hand-coded websites instead of WordPress? +

Hand-coded websites load in under 0.5 seconds, are more secure, GDPR-compliant and preferred by Google and AI assistants. No plugin risks, no subscriptions, the source code is yours.

Is my website optimised for Google and AI search? +

Yes. Every website is built with Schema.org structured data, semantic HTML and optimised load times — so Google, ChatGPT and voice assistants display your content first.
